Munich Quantum Software Forum

Quantum computing is becoming a reality and, with recent accomplishments, software for this promising technology is becoming key for successful utilization. Numerous players introduce new software solutions frequently.

The MQSF brings together the “who’s who” in quantum computing software. By connecting leading researchers, developers, and decision makers, the forum fosters the exchange of ideas, showcases state-of-the-art software solutions, and facilitates discussions about the future direction of the quantum software ecosystem.

You can find the full program here.

In addition to the talks, the forum will feature almost 50 software pitches showcasing new tools and features. Each tool will be:

  • Presented in a rapid on-stage session, and

  • Featured on a poster during the event breaks.

Coffee, snacks, and drinks will be available throughout the event, and a warm lunch will be provided for all registered attendees.

You will find plenty of opportunities to connect with fellow quantum software experts: meet old acquaintances, brainstorm new ideas, relax, hack, and explore collaborations together throughout the forum.

Please Note:
Until now, MQSF has been free of charge for participants, largely thanks to funding from research projects and substantial investments by the organizers. While this model allowed us to establish and grow the event, it is unfortunately not sustainable in the long term. To ensure that MQSF can continue to bring together the quantum community in a professional and sustainable way, we have expanded the event's funding model. The largest contribution now comes from our sponsors, whose generous support makes MQSF possible in the first place. We are sincerely grateful for their commitment (check out the sponsors).

In addition, we have introduced a modest registration fee of €99 (covering access to the event, the venue, drinks, lunches, and refreshments during breaks). Besides helping to cover a portion of the event costs, the fee also reduces no-show registrations and allows us to plan the event more reliably. We are grateful for your support in helping us keep MQSF a sustainable and high-quality community event.

Tickets are available on a first-come, first-served basis. Demand has consistently exceeded our capacity, so we are pleased to announce that we have been able to increase the venue capacity once again this year. Nevertheless, once the available ticket allocation has been reached, further interested participants will be placed on a waiting list. Please make sure to register as soon as possible, and no later than September 20, after which late registration fees may apply.

Transportation

From Munich Central Station (Hauptbahnhof): Take any westbound (S1, S2, S3, S4, S5, S6, or S8) S-Bahn and get off at Hirschgarten. The venue is a short walk from the station.

From Munich Airport (MUC): Take either the S1 or S8 S-Bahn towards the city center and get off at Hirschgarten. The venue is within walking distance of the station.
