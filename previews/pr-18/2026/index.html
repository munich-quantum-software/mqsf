Full program

The forum featured 44 software pitches, with short stage presentations and posters during the breaks.

Coffee, snacks, and drinks were available throughout the event, and a warm lunch was provided for all registered attendees.

Venue & Event Information

Registration Closed

The two-day registration fee was €99, including lunches and refreshments. Sponsors provided the largest contribution to the event’s funding.

The event was organized by MQSC, with support from TUM’s Chair for Design Automation and Munich Quantum Valley.

Location

The forum took place at the Backstage Kulturzentrum (Halle) in Munich.

Backstage Kulturzentrum
Reitknechtstraße 6
80639 Munich

Transportation

From Munich Central Station (Hauptbahnhof): Take any westbound (S1, S2, S3, S4, S5, S6, or S8) S-Bahn and get off at Hirschgarten. The venue is a short walk from the station.

From Munich Airport (MUC): Take either the S1 or S8 S-Bahn towards the city center and get off at Hirschgarten. The venue is within walking distance of the station.

Accomodation

Munich offers stays for every budget. The following hotels are among the closest to the venue.

  • Budget: Rent-a-Room Munich

  • Mid-range: Holiday Inn Express Munich City West

  • Upscale: Munich Marriott Hotel City West
